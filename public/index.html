  2. Receive your quoteWe analyse word count and challenges, then send a quote (per word or page) with our terms and POPIA statement.

Track Changes guide

Viewing changes

Open the document in Microsoft Word or a similar editor, then click the Review tab. You can toggle tracking on and off to see the proposed edits.

Accepting changes

Click a change, then choose Accept in the Review tab. “Accept all” is available in the drop-down, but we advise against it: you may accept changes you have not read.

Rejecting changes

Click a change you disagree with and choose Reject. The drop-down also offers “Reject all changes”.

Reviewing comments

Click a margin comment to read our note. When you have dealt with it, right-click and choose Delete comment.

Translation is R0.35 per word and editing R0.25 per word, and specialist work is priced per word in the fee structure below. The final price depends on length and complexity. The minimum fee is R150.
